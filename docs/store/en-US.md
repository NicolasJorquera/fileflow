## Full description
FileFlow is a PDF toolbox that works on your phone. Your documents are never uploaded: no accounts, no cloud, no waiting.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Compress: pick a target size (say, under 2 MB for a form) and FileFlow tells you how big the file will be before it starts.
• Merge PDFs: combine several files into one and drag to reorder.
• Organize pages: move, rotate, delete or extract pages, or split a PDF into parts.
• PDF to images: save pages as JPG or PNG.
• Photos to PDF: turn gallery photos into a PDF with the page size you need.
• Scan documents: use Google's scanner to capture paper with automatic cropping and cleanup.

PRIVATE FOR REAL
• Everything is processed on the phone, even in airplane mode.
• Results are kept in Recent and deleted automatically after the period you choose.
• No sign-up and no camera or storage permissions: you pick every file.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE
• Large buttons and clear wording.
• Works with TalkBack and 200 % text.
• In English, Spanish and Portuguese, with light and dark themes.

FileFlow is free and supported by ads, which never appear over your documents.
